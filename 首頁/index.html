設計理念 Design Statement

我的創作聚焦於「感知如何被轉譯為體驗」。
我習慣從日常感受與情緒狀態出發，結合互動技術、視覺設計與聲音，將抽象的感受轉化為可被觸發、回饋與共感的體驗形式。在設計過程中，我不將技術視為目的，而是作為放大感受的媒介。
透過互動、動態與生成系統，我嘗試讓使用者在短時間內建立直覺理解，並在參與的過程中感受到節奏、層次與情緒的流動。
我關注設計如何在理性結構與感性體驗之間取得平衡，
讓作品不只是被觀看，而是能被觸碰、被回應，並留下記憶。

My work focuses on translating perception into experience.
I begin with everyday sensations and emotional states, combining interaction, visual design, and sound to transform abstract feelings into experiences that can be triggered, responded to, and shared.

I do not treat technology as an end in itself, but as a medium that amplifies sensation.
Through interaction, motion, and generative systems, I aim to create intuitive experiences that can be understood within moments, while allowing users to feel rhythm, layers, and emotional flow through participation.

I am interested in the balance between rational structure and emotional experience,
designing works that are not only seen, but touched, responded to, and remembered.
